# t@sk4ce guide for parents

t@sk4ce helps your kids get their chores done. When chores are approved, the internet comes on. This guide is for parents. You do not need to be good with computers.

Read the pages in order the first time. It takes about 20 minutes.

1. [What you need](01-what-you-need.md): the small computer and router.
2. [Install](02-install.md): put t@sk4ce on the small computer.
3. [First-time setup](03-first-setup.md): add your family, kids and devices.
4. [Everyday use](04-everyday-use.md): chores, approvals, screen time, rewards, chat.
5. [For kids](05-for-kids.md): one page to show your kids.
6. [Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md): when something looks wrong.
7. [FAQ](07-faq.md): quick answers.

Boxes that say **For the curious** are for people who want the technical detail. You can skip them.

Words we use:

- **The home box**: the small always-on computer running t@sk4ce.
- **Paused**: a device cannot use the internet right now.
- **Needs you**: the list of things waiting for your decision.
